AirBack Privacy Policy

Canonical copy: airback.io/privacy.html. Terms: airback.io/terms.html. Support: hi@airback.io.

Effective October 31, 2026. Applies to the AirBack app for Google TV and Android TV, package com.airback, published by deJesus.biz.

Summary

AirBack runs entirely on your local network. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What the app does with data

  • Mirrored and cast content (screen, audio, video, photos) is received over your Wi-Fi network from a device you control, decoded on the TV, and shown on the screen. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Device name: AirBack reads the name you gave your TV in Android settings and advertises it on your local network so your other devices can find the receiver. This stays on your network.
  • Settings you choose in the app are stored on the TV only.
  • Diagnostic logs are kept on the TV and shown in the app's Logs tab. They leave the device only if you choose to export and share them yourself.

Permissions

AirBack asks for network access (to receive on the local network), the ability to keep the device awake while a session is active, and "Display over other apps" so the receiver can come forward when a device connects. None of these are used to collect data.

Third parties

AirBack contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's own policies.

Children

AirBack is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Open source

AirBack is free software under the GPL-3.0 license. The complete source code is at github.com/deJesusbiz/airback, so anyone can verify this policy against what the app does.

Contact

Questions: hi@airback.io. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Política de privacidad de AirBack

Vigente desde el 31 de octubre de 2026.

AirBack funciona por completo en tu red local. No recopila, guarda ni transmite datos personales al desarrollador ni a terceros. No hay cuentas, ni analítica, ni publicidad, ni rastreo. El contenido que duplicas o envías se decodifica en la TV y se muestra en pantalla; no se graba ni se envía a ningún sitio. El nombre de tu TV se anuncia solo dentro de tu red para que tus dispositivos encuentren el receptor. Los ajustes y los registros de diagnóstico se guardan en la TV y solo salen de ella si tú decides exportarlos. La app no incluye SDK de publicidad ni de analítica. El código fuente completo está en github.com/deJesusbiz/airback. Contacto: hi@airback.io.
