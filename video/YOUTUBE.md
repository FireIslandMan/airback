## Description (EN first, then ES)

AirBack turns a Google TV or Android TV into an AirPlay receiver. Mirror an iPhone, iPad or Mac. No ads, no subscription, no spinner when the TV is asleep or on another input. When you stop mirroring, the TV goes back to whatever it was showing: live TV, the HDMI input, the app you had open.

The iPhone close-up and the living-room shot of the TV switching back to a match are AI-generated illustrations (Google Veo). Every other shot is a real capture from the TV and the Mac.

Honest limits: Netflix, Apple TV+ and Disney+ block every third-party AirPlay receiver on the sending side (DRM). Everything else works: your screen, photos, your own videos, YouTube, music, presentations.
