Why another receiver

The free ones make you pay in other ways Los gratuitos te cobran de otra forma

Ad-supported AirPlay receivers interrupt you, spin forever when the TV is on another input, and leave the TV stuck on their idle screen when you're done.

Ad-supported receivers

  • Full-screen ads before and after every session
  • Your iPhone shows a spinner when the TV is asleep or on cable
  • Disconnect, and the TV sits on their logo until you grab the remote
  • A subscription to remove the ads

AirBack

  • No ads. Ever. One price.
  • Comes forward on connect, even from the screensaver or another input
  • Steps back on disconnect, so the TV returns to live TV or the app you had open
  • Shows up under your TV's own name with nothing to configure

What it does

Mirror. Watch. Get your TV back. Duplica. Mira. Recupera tu TV.

↩︎

Auto-restore on disconnect

When the last sender drops, AirBack steps back to whatever was showing before: the HDMI input, YouTube, the home screen. Al desconectar, la TV vuelve a lo que estaba mostrando.

📺

Your TV's own name

It advertises the name you gave the TV in Android settings, so your devices already know it. Aparece con el nombre de tu TV.

⚡

Wakes the screen

Connect while the TV is on the screensaver and the picture is up in under a second. Despierta la pantalla al conectar.

🖥

iPhone, iPad and Mac

Screen mirroring and AirPlay video from any Apple device on the same Wi-Fi. Desde cualquier dispositivo Apple en la misma red.

🔒

Nothing leaves your network

No account, no analytics, no advertising SDK. The source is public, so you can check. Sin cuenta, sin analítica, sin anuncios.

🇵🇷

English and Spanish

The whole app, both languages, from day one. Toda la app en inglés y español.

Honest limits Límites, sin letra pequeña

Netflix, Apple TV+ and Disney+ block every third-party AirPlay receiver on the sending side (DRM). Nothing AirBack can do about that. Everything else works: your screen, photos, your own videos, YouTube, music, presentations. Your TV and your Apple device need to be on the same network.

Netflix, Apple TV+ y Disney+ bloquean todo receptor AirPlay de terceros (DRM). Lo demás funciona: tu pantalla, fotos, tus videos, YouTube, música, presentaciones.

How it works

Three taps on the iPhone Tres toques en el iPhone

1

Install AirBack on the TV

From Google Play. Allow "Display over other apps" when asked, once. Instálalo desde Google Play.

2

Control Center → Screen Mirroring

Pick your TV by name. Centro de control → Duplicar pantalla → tu TV.

3

Stop when you're done

The TV goes back to what it was showing. Al terminar, la TV vuelve sola.
