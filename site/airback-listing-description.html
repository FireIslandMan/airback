AirBack turns a Google TV or Android TV into an AirPlay receiver for your iPhone, iPad and Mac. No ads, no subscription, no account. When you stop mirroring, the TV goes back to whatever it was showing: live TV, the HDMI input, the app you had open.

  • Shows up under your TV's own name. Nothing to configure.
  • Comes forward when a device connects, even from the screensaver or another input.
  • Steps back when the last device disconnects.
  • English and Spanish. Open source under GPL-3.0.

Honest limit: Netflix, Apple TV+ and Disney+ block every third-party AirPlay receiver (DRM). Everything else works: your screen, photos, your own videos, YouTube, music, presentations.

$3.99, once, on Google Play. Built in Fajardo, Puerto Rico.
